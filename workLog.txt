Entry 1

Task: Plan the website.

Decision: I decided to create a fictional car dealership website 
with three pages: home, about and contact.

The plan: 
The home page will introduce the dealership, its vehicles and main services.
The about page will present the dealership's history, values and services.
The contact page will provide contact information and a simple contact form.

Entry 2

Task: structure the available vehicles section.

Decision:
I decided to create three <article> elements inside the vehicles section,
with each article representing one available car. Each vehicle will have
its own information, such as model, price, year and mileage.

I chose <article> because each vehicle can be understood as an independent
piece of content inside the section.

Entry 3

Task: structure the available services section.

Decision: 
I decided to use an unordered list (<ul>) to display the three 
services offered by the dealership. I chose an unordered list because
the services do not have a specific order of priority. 

Entry 4

Task: Structure the about page and add consistent footer.

Decision: 
A simple page featuring a summary of the store's history and an overview
of the shopping experience. I also decided to include a footer on all three
pages with a link to the contact page.

Entry 5

Task: I needed to create the contact page.

Problem + solution: 
It was my first time using a form and structuring a contact page, so I spent
a good amount of time going through the HTML Forms documentation
provided for the assignment, and it was very helpful.

Entry 6 

Task: Create the contact structure.

Decision: 
Stick to what works- keeping it simple. Since this was my first time 
working with an HTML form, I opted for a simple yet comprehensive field that meets
the user's needs withing the scope of my project.
